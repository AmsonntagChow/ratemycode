# InboxPilot founder brief

InboxPilot is a working demo that drafts replies for independent real-estate agents. The founder says agents hate email, the market is enormous, and the product could become the operating system for every property transaction.

Current evidence:

- 42 people joined a waitlist after a founder post.
- 7 friends watched a demo; 6 said they would try it.
- No one has connected a real inbox.
- There are no repeated-use, retention, payment, conversion, acquisition-cost, or serving-cost measurements.
- The founder expects referrals to drive growth but has not run a referral experiment.

The demo can draft a plausible reply from a pasted email. It does not yet connect to email providers, enforce an approval flow, or measure whether users send the draft.
